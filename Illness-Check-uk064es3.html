How the score works
Baseline
Average of the 7 nights before last night, last night excluded.
Deviation
Change in the direction that signals illness, divided by each metric's full scale, clamped to 0–1. A metric is flagged above 0.35 (the tick on each bar).
Score
Weighted average of the available metrics (RHR 30, HRV 25, respiration 25, SpO2 20), divided by the weights used; ×0.5 with no flags, ×0.6 with one, ×1.0 with two, ×1.15 with three, ×1.3 with four; capped at 100.
Bands
0–24 Clear · 25–44 Watch · 45–64 Elevated · 65–84 High · 85–100 Very likely.
Unavailable
Missing last night, or fewer than 4 nights in the last 15. Each greyed-out card says why.
Source
—, pulled each morning by a scheduled Claude task.
